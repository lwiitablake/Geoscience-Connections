## Longer-term graph model

Separate disciplines, geoscience concepts, concrete activities, cases, and sources into stable node types. Add explicit relationship types and evidence at the relationship level. A shared word or shared source should not automatically create a direct scientific relationship. Keep exploratory links explicitly unverified and separately controllable. Investigate one family at a time, then review cross-family links. Preserve source review scope and publication/access dates.

## Layout judgment

A stable clustered layout is a useful next step for this collection: related disciplines can sit near family anchors while shared sources occupy nearby space. It avoids a constantly moving simulation. This is a design judgment, not a usability-study result. As the graph grows, overview clustering, search, and focused neighborhoods will matter more than fitting every label on screen. Current positions are organizational; distance and proximity are not evidence of scientific similarity.
